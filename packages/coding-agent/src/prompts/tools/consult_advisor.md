Talk to the live advisor on a persistent conversation thread. Use when an advisory is unclear, contested, incomplete, or you need the advisor's rationale or a revised stance before acting or escalating to the user.

- Continues the same advisor thread across calls and across passive reviews — do not restate full history.
- When multiple advisors are active, pass `advisor` (name or slug).
- Prefer consult over guessing the advisor's intent; prefer consult over `ask` when the question is about the advisory itself.
- The tool result is the advisor's answer; weigh it like any other advisory (guidance still applies to prior cards).
- Unavailable when the advisor is disabled or no advisor model is assigned.
